- **Correct Answer:** **Option D**

#### Psychometric Rationale & Source Grounding
At each step of generation, the model computes probabilities for thousands of possible next tokens. #### Distractor Analysis & Operational Traps
- **Option A Flaw:** Different compute clusters run the identical frozen model weights and deterministic sampling algorithms.
- **Option B Flaw:** The prompt was identical in both runs, so contradictory operator inputs could not explain the divergence.
