Source Grounding & Rationale: AI access is not AI fluency. Two individuals using the exact same model achieve vastly different results because fluency is defined by human competency: working effectively (achieving the goal), efficiently (conserving time and tokens), ethically (transparent and fair), and safely (protecting privacy and institutional data).
