## PART 2: ANSWER KEY & PSYCHOMETRIC DISSECTIONS

### Question 1 — Evaluation & Analysis
- **Target Domain:** Domain 1: Prompting and Task Execution
- **Sub-Topic Grounding:** Core AI Fluency: Effective, Efficient, Ethical, Safe (Part 1, Section 1: AI access is not AI fluency)
- **Correct Answer:** **Option C**

#### Psychometric Rationale & Source Grounding
AI access is not AI fluency. Two individuals using the exact same model achieve vastly different results because fluency is defined by human competency: working effectively (achieving the goal), efficiently (conserving time and tokens), ethically (transparent and fair), and safely (protecting privacy and institutional data).

#### Distractor Analysis & Operational Traps
- **Option A Flaw:** Fluency depends on clear problem definition and verification, not secret tokens or bypassing safety guardrails.
- **Option B Flaw:** Compute allocation is uniform across subscription tiers and does not adjust based on user error logs.
- **Option D Flaw:** Models do not adjust statistical accuracy or confidence based on user organizational status.

==================================================

### Question 2 — Evaluation & Analysis
- **Target Domain:** Domain 1: Prompting and Task Execution
- **Sub-Topic Grounding:** Stateless Nature & Working Memory vs Human Learning (Opening: Imagine a talented new colleague joins your team)
- **Correct Answer:** **Option A**

#### Psychometric Rationale & Source Grounding
Unlike human colleagues who retain memory across weeks, foundation models carry nothing from one chat session into the next. Each new conversation starts completely stateless with read-only frozen weights, requiring instructions and formatting rules to be re-supplied or stored in persistent Project knowledge.

#### Distractor Analysis & Operational Traps
- **Option B Flaw:** The reversion is caused by lack of conversational continuity, not conflict resolution among clinical guidelines.
- **Option C Flaw:** Session resets are an inherent architectural property of stateless models, not an administrative server purge.
- **Option D Flaw:** Tokenizers are static lookup tables and do not selectively purge specialized vocabulary branches.

==================================================

### Question 3 — Evaluation & Analysis
- **Target Domain:** Domain 1: Prompting and Task Execution
- **Sub-Topic Grounding:** Economic Efficiency & Token Conservation (Part 1, Section 1: Effective, Efficient, Ethical, Safe)
- **Correct Answer:** **Option D**

#### Psychometric Rationale & Source Grounding
Efficiency requires minimizing wasted time, human effort, and tokens. Ingesting 300-page raw agreements repeatedly for unfocused queries consumes millions of unnecessary input tokens, inflates enterprise billing, and degrades output precision through context overcrowding.
